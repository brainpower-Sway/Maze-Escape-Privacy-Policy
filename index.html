Maze Escape — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Maze Escape is a simple maze puzzle game made for entertainment and casual gameplay. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the game experience safe, transparent, and simple. This page explains what data the app touches, what device permissions it requests, and why.

1Information We Collect

Maze Escape does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or home address to play the game.

The app does process some non-personal technical information on-device and, where noted below, through Google's advertising service:

  • Gameplay statistics — levels completed, time played, stars earned, streaks. Stored only on your device to track your progress.
  • Basic device/technical information — device type and general usage patterns, used to serve and measure ads (see Section 3).
  • Advertising identifiers — such as your device's Advertising ID, collected by Google AdMob (see Section 3).

2How We Use Information

Any information processed may be used to:

  • Save and restore your local game progress, settings, and streaks
  • Improve game performance, functionality, and stability
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Understand general, aggregate gameplay usage
  • Serve and personalize advertisements (see Section 3)

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

3Advertising

Maze Escape displays ads through Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general usage information to show ads and measure their performance, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Ads Policy.

You can control whether you see personalized ads from within the app, under Me → Privacy Preferences. Turning this off requests non-personalized ads where supported, though some data may still be processed by Google to serve and measure ads and to detect fraud.

New installs also get an ads-free period for the first few levels so you can try the game before any ad is shown.

4App Permissions

Maze Escape requests the following device permissions. None of them are used to access your personal files, contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

PermissionUsed for
INTERNET Loading ads and the daily shared maze challenge.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checking network availability before requesting an ad.
VIBRATE Optional haptic feedback on wall bumps and level wins (can be turned off in Settings).
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows a once-a-day local reminder (e.g. to keep your Daily Challenge streak alive) if you haven't played yet that day. This is scheduled and generated entirely on your device — no data is sent to us or anyone else to show it. Can be turned off any time in Settings, and is only requested on Android 13+ where the system requires explicit permission to show notifications.
AD_ID / Privacy Sandbox APIs
(Topics, Attribution, Ad ID)		 Automatically included by Google's advertising SDK (AdMob / Google Play services) to serve, measure, and personalize ads, and to support Android's Privacy Sandbox. We do not use these directly ourselves.
WAKE_LOCK / FOREGROUND_SERVICE Standard internal permissions required by Google's advertising SDK for background ad-loading tasks. Not used by the game directly.

5Cookies and Similar Technologies

The app itself does not use browser cookies. The third-party advertising service it uses (Google AdMob) may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads and understand how users interact with them.

6Data Storage & Security

Your game progress, settings (sound, music, theme, D-Pad style), and stats are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage. We do not operate a server that stores your personal gameplay data. We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game; however, no online service can guarantee complete security.

7Children's Privacy

Maze Escape is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the game is used by children, we recommend disabling ad personalization via Privacy Preferences.

8Your Choices

  • Manage ad personalization any time in the app: Me → Privacy Preferences.
  • Turn Sound, Music, Vibration, or Notifications on or off in Me → Settings.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising generally through your device's ad settings (Android Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Revoke the notification permission at any time from your device's system App Settings for Maze Escape.

9Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its features change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page, and the in-app Privacy Rights screen will reflect the same information.
